# API cost estimate

Generated by `api/evals/cost_model.py` (input sizes estimated at ~4 characters per token). Prices: `claude-sonnet-5-5` $2.00 input, $10.00 output, $0.20 cache read and $2.50 cache write per million tokens.

## Per action

| Action | Model calls | Cached prefix (tokens) | Dynamic input (tokens) | Output incl. thinking (tokens) | Cost, likely |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| AI drill grade | 1 | 950 | 209–2,430 | 1,200–5,000 | $0.0291 |
| Tutor question | 2 | 471 (under the 512-token cache minimum) | 522–6,300 | 330–3,200 | $0.0108 |
| Coach rewrite | 1 | 237 (under the 512-token cache minimum) | 61–361 | 250–900 | $0.0052 |

## Worst case per learner

The cost governor caps each learner at 200,000 tokens a day, so one learner can never cost more than about **$2.00 a day** (if every token were output), and about $0.80 at a typical 3:1 input-to-output mix.

## Findings

- **Output dominates.** About 89% of a likely grading call is output and thinking. The first lever to measure is grader effort: re-run the live eval at `low` and keep it only if agreement and the safety gate hold.
- **Caching does not apply to: Tutor question, Coach rewrite.** Their stable prefixes are under the 512-token minimum, so `cache_control` there has no effect. They are cheap anyway; do not pad prompts to reach the minimum.
- **The live CI eval can run at roughly half price through the Message Batches API** (it is not latency-sensitive), but the server-side `fallbacks` parameter is rejected on batches, so batch runs need their own refusal handling.

## Assumptions to replace with measurements

- Output tokens include adaptive thinking, which is billed as output and is the largest uncertainty. The grader runs at effort `medium`, the tutor and coach at `low`.
- Cache hit rates per scenario are assumptions; verify with `cache_read_input_tokens` in usage.
- Standby (`claude-haiku-4-5`) traffic at rung 2 costs about half and is ignored here; server-side refusal fallbacks are rare and also ignored.
- Hosting is not included (API host, Postgres, identity provider): small demos fit free or entry tiers; check current provider pricing.
